## Bloomberg: Geo Compute Team Lead (Jun 2022 – Feb 2026)
- What did the team build or ship?
- What was the impact?
- Anything you're particularly proud of?

**Your notes:**
- Founded this team from scratch and built a new business line within Bloomberg from zero
- Bloomberg had a MAP function, but it expected clients to come to MAP and do analysis there. The insight: what if we create an API-first product with the premise of finding the geographical footprint for any financial entity?
  - Company? Where are the facilities and supply chain?
  - Muni bond? Where is the investment happening?
- Once you have the footprint, you can reason with alternative geospatial data. Example: "Is this storm going to affect Exxon?" Exxon → where the refineries are, storm → where the forecast will hit. Now you have your answer.
- You can go back historically (which you can't do with a map — a map is point-in-time by nature). With this API, you can construct scores.
- Compare company footprints: how's Aldi doing vs Target? Demographics for a footprint — how do they compare in capturing low median income areas?
- Exposure analysis: has biodiversity increased in this area? Does it have something to do with the factories in the area?
